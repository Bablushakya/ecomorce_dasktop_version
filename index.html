Frequently Asked Questions

How are your laddoos sweetened without refined sugar? +

We use only two clean, whole sweeteners: 100% natural Medjool dates and raw, unrefined chemical-free jaggery. There is zero white cane sugar, zero high fructose corn syrup, and zero artificial sweeteners in any recipe.

What are the delivery rules and timelines? +

Our standard delivery time is 24–48 hours to guarantee kitchen freshness.
• Within Dwarka: Orders above ₹500 get FREE DELIVERY.
• Outside Dwarka: Orders are dispatched via Porter, and the customer pays the actual Porter delivery charge directly at dispatch/delivery without any markup.

What is the shelf life and how should I store the laddoos? +

Shelf life is 45 days from the date of dispatch. Store at room temperature in an airtight container away from direct sunlight and heat. Because we do not use chemical preservatives, keeping them sealed preserves their aromatic Desi Ghee flavor.

Are the glass jars reusable? +

Yes! Our traditional glass jars are heavy-duty, food-grade, airtight glass decanters with gold airtight seals. They can be reused indefinitely in your kitchen for dry fruits, spices, or tea leaves.

What fat source is used in the laddoos? +

We use authentic, slow-churned Desi Ghee. We strictly reject hydrogenated vegetable fats, vanaspati, and palm oil.
